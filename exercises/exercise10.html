UMass English major with a love of writing and nature

Hello, I'm Laura Frame. I am currently at University of Massachusetts-Amherst as an English major. I have mostly worked in public facing positions such as restaurant, food retail and real estate. I retired from full-time customer service work in order to finish my bachelor's degree and pursue work in writing, which has always been one of my great loves. I am most interested in combining my other interests with my writing skills to create both fiction and nonfiction pieces involving the environment, organic gardening at home, native planting, pollinators, and convenient but healthy home cooking.

I am also interested in other media projects such as podcasts or videos. My goal is to combine what I enjoy doing with sharing my skillset for the greater good. Even if it's "just" helping people learn how to make simple, easy food in a relatable, fun way, I think it's a great project! I am also interested in nature and wildlife photography and although I am currently an amateur, I am always learning and working to expand my skills.

I have a huge love for cats and am always inspired by them in many different projects. I have a well-rounded knonwledge of cats, including feline behavior, and am planning to design a line of cat toys that are less dangerous than most toys that are currently sold in big box stores. It's very sad how many toys I see that are marketed as fun but are actually very dangerous! (Hint: never buy toys with feathers. They are a choking hazard.) I have a cat bed design project on the back burner right now as I focus in finishing my degree.
