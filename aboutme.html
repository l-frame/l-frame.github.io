Who Am I?

Hi! I am from Long Island, New York. I relocated to the Pioneer Valley in early 2025 for change of pace and went back to school full time to earn my BA in English.

I am interested in combining my other interests with my writing skills.

I want to create both fiction and nonfiction pieces involving the environment, organic gardening at home, native planting, pollinators, and convenient but healthy home cooking.

I am also interested in other media projects such as podcasts or videos.

My goal is to combine what I enjoy doing with sharing my skillset for the greater good. Even if it's "just" helping people learn how to make simple, easy food in a relatable, fun way.

I think it's a great project! I am also interested in nature and wildlife photography and although I am currently an amateur, I am always expanding my skills.

I love cats! My three cats are a huge part of my life and are always inspiring me in different ways, from designing more sustainable and economical cat beds to designing a unique form of poetry.

I have a well-rounded knowledge of cats, including feline behavior, and am planning to design a line of cat toys.





A few of my interests are:

  • editing
  • prose fiction
  • screenwriting
  • creative nonfiction
  • wildlife and nature photopgrahy
  • history of nature and animals in literature
